1. Introduction

Welcome to RACS Projects ("Company", "we", "our", or "us"). We are an integrated Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing (MEP), Fire Fighting, and BMS/ELV engineering and contracting consultancy based in Hyderabad, Telangana, India.

We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal and professional information you share with us through our website (https://racsprojects.com/), consultation channels, project portals, and communication tools. This Privacy Policy explains our policies regarding the collection, storage, use, and protection of your data.

Core Commitment

RACS Projects does not sell, rent, or trade client or user personal information to third-party marketers. All collected data is processed strictly for engineering service delivery, project management, customer support, and legitimate business operations.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information directly from you when you interact with our website, request project quotes, schedule site visits, or communicate with our engineering consultants:

A. Personal & Business Identification Information
  • Contact Information: Full name, official email address, phone number(s), and business mailing address.
  • Project Inquiries: Organization name, project location, building type (Commercial, Residential, Industrial, Healthcare), MEP service specifications, budget estimates, and CAD/BOQ document uploads.
  • Communication Records: Transcripts of emails, WhatsApp messages, contact form submissions, and phone calls.
B. Automatically Collected Technical Data
  • Device & Usage Data: Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, operating system, preferred language, access timestamps, and referring URLs.
  • Navigation Analytics: Pages visited, time spent on individual service pages, click paths, and interaction data captured via Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to support and enhance our engineering services, client relationships, and business operations, including:

  • Engineering Consultations & Quotes: Preparing detailed MEP bill of quantities (BOQ), technical proposals, HVAC/Electrical heat load calculations, and site audit reports.
  • Project Execution & Site Management: Coordinating site inspections, material procurement, testing, commissioning, and annual maintenance contract (AMC) support.
  • Client Support: Responding to service inquiries, technical feedback, and urgent MEP maintenance calls.
  • Operational Improvements: Analyzing website performance and user engagement to enhance navigation and technical content clarity.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Fulfilling statutory requirements, tax documentation, Indian Standards (IS), NBC, and TAC compliance obligations.

4. Legal Basis for Processing Data

We process your personal information under the following legal frameworks:

  • Contractual Obligation: Processing necessary for the performance of a contract or preliminary project quotation requested by you.
  • Legitimate Interests: Necessary for conducting commercial engineering operations, fraud prevention, and improving service safety standards.
  • Consent: Where you have granted explicit consent for direct communication or specific analytics cookies.
  • Statutory Compliance: Compliance with applicable laws in India, including the Information Technology Act, 2000, Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, and municipal construction regulations.

5. Data Protection & Security Standards

We implement industry-standard physical, electronic, and managerial safeguards to protect your personal and confidential project information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

  • Encryption: Data transmitted via our website is secured using Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure (HTTPS) and SSL/TLS encryption.
  • Access Control: Access to project records and client contact details is strictly restricted to authorized engineering personnel and project managers.
  • Secure Servers: Hosting infrastructures are configured with firewall protections, malware monitoring, and periodic security evaluations.

6. Cookies & Web Analytics

Our website uses cookies and related web measurement technologies to optimize browsing experience and measure site performance.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for fundamental website functionality, navigation, and mobile menu features.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: We utilize Google Analytics (GA4) and Google Tag Manager to collect aggregated, anonymized usage metrics, such as page views and session durations.
  • Managing Cookies: You can choose to disable cookies through your web browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect certain interactive elements of our platform.

7. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not monetize your personal data. We share information only under the following limited circumstances:

  • Authorized Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors (such as web hosting providers, email servers, and IT service providers) operating under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Subcontractors & Equipment Suppliers: Project-specific details may be shared with approved MEP OEM manufacturers (e.g., HVAC units, Fire Pumps, Switchgears) solely for equipment ordering and warranty registrations.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by court order, law enforcement request, or statutory regulation under Indian jurisdiction.

8. User Rights & Data Retention

Depending on applicable regulations, you have rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access & Correction: Right to request a copy of your personal data and rectify any inaccuracies.
  • Deletion / Erasure: Right to request erasure of your data, subject to statutory tax and engineering audit retention requirements.
  • Data Retention: Inquiry data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill project requirements, resolve technical inquiries, or adhere to legal record-keeping statutes.

9. Third-Party Links & Websites

Our website may contain links to external web resources (such as Google Maps, partner developer portals, social media channels, or third-party manufacturer catalogs). RACS Projects is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of these external sites. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies upon visiting.

10. Policy Amendments & Updates

RACS Projects reserves the right to modify or update this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect changing legal requirements, industry practices, or service offerings. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of the policy.
