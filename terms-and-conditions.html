2. Scope of MEP Engineering Services

RACS Projects provides turnkey design, engineering consultancy, supply, installation, testing, commissioning, and maintenance services across five core MEP divisions:

  • Fire Fighting Systems: Hydrant systems, automatic sprinkler networks, fire alarm panels, gas suppression systems, and NOC compliance audits.
  • HVAC & Ventilation: Chilled water systems, VRF/VRV units, clean room HVAC, basement ventilation, and ductwork engineering.
  • Electrical Systems: HT/LT sub-stations, transformers, DG sets, power distribution panels, internal wiring, and earthing protection.
  • Plumbing & Public Health Engineering: Water supply networks, drainage, sewage treatment plants (STP), rainwater harvesting, and sanitary fixtures.
  • BMS & ELV Systems: Building Management Systems, CCTV surveillance, access control, public address systems, and structured networking cabling.

Specific project scopes, technical deliverables, execution timelines, and commercial terms are finalized through individual Work Orders or Contracts executed between RACS Projects and the Client.

4. Proposals, Quotations & Contracts

Guidelines governing engineering estimates and commercial contracts:

  • Validity Period: Commercial quotations and tenders issued by RACS Projects are valid for thirty (30) days from issuance, unless explicitly stated otherwise in writing.
  • Price Variations: Rates for copper, steel, PVC, refrigerants, and imported equipment are subject to market variations prior to formal work order placement.
  • Formal Execution: A contract is formally established only upon receipt of a signed Work Order (WO) / Purchase Order (PO) and the agreed initial advance payment.

5. Client Responsibilities & Site Obligations

To ensure timely and safe project execution, the Client agrees to:

  • Provide unhindered, safe access to the project site, clear working fronts, and necessary storage facilities for tools and MEP materials.
  • Supply temporary electricity, water, and scaffolding required during installation and testing phases as per contract agreements.
  • Obtain necessary civil site readiness, architectural approvals, and structural clearances prior to MEP equipment installation.
  • Ensure prompt review and approval of engineering drawings, material submittals, and milestone completion certificates.

6. Payment Terms & Invoicing

Standard financial provisions for engineering and contracting projects:

  • Milestone Payments: Invoices are issued in progressive stages (Advance, Material Dispatch, Installation, Testing & Commissioning) specified in the Work Order.
  • Payment Schedule: Payments must be remitted within seven (7) to fourteen (14) days of invoice date via wire transfer or cheque to RACS Projects official account.
  • Taxes: All prices are subject to applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST) in accordance with Indian tax laws.
  • Delayed Payments: Overdue invoices may attract interest charges at 1.5% per month or the maximum permissible legal rate until full settlement.

7. Warranties & Defect Liability Period (DLP)

RACS Projects provides a standard twelve (12) month Defect Liability Period (DLP) from the date of handover/commissioning for installation workmanship.

  • OEM Equipment Warranties: Equipment warranties (chillers, pumps, DG sets, panels) are provided directly by original equipment manufacturers and governed by OEM warranty conditions.
  • Warranty Exclusions: DLP warranty does not cover damages arising from misuse, improper operation by client staff, unauthorized modifications, voltage fluctuations, or failure to perform routine maintenance.
