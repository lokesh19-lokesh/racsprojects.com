Who We Are

Pioneering Complete Building Engineering Solutions

RACS Projects is a premier MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing) contracting and engineering firm headquartered in Hyderabad, India. We deliver end-to-end design, procurement, installation, testing, commissioning, and facility management services across commercial, residential, industrial, and infrastructure sectors.

Founded with a vision to raise engineering standards in the built environment, our multidisciplinary team of licensed engineers, technical project managers, and certified technicians brings deep technical knowledge to every project. We integrate cutting-edge building management technologies with energy-efficient systems to lower operational costs and maximize safety.

Workflow Lifecycle
